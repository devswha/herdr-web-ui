### Added
- A workspace's menu and the command palette now offer "Resume Oh My Pi session…": it lists the
  earlier Oh My Pi sessions of the workspace's folder (title, first message, last write) and resumes
  the one you pick exactly (`omp --resume <session file>`) in a new tab, or brings forward the pane
  that already has it. Sessions open in a pane are not listed. This PC only.
